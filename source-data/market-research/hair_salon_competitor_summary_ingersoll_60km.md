# Hair Salon Competitor Pricing: Ingersoll, ON 60 km Radius

Research date: 2026-05-16  
Search area: Ingersoll, Ontario plus nearby Woodstock, Tillsonburg, London, St Thomas, and Stratford.

## What I Found

I found 10 usable competitor records with public pricing. The strongest local pricing comparables are Woodstock and Tillsonburg because they are closest to Ingersoll. London adds a broader price spread, including budget student pricing, textured-hair specialists, and higher-service salons.

## Closest Direct Comparables

Tranquility Spa + Salon in Tillsonburg publishes a low-to-mid local menu: shampoo/cut/style starts at $40+, roots at $55+, full colour at $65+, and full foil at $120+.

Hairpower in Woodstock is another close benchmark: women’s cut $48+, men’s cut $30+, colour $60-$80, partial highlights $115+, and full head highlights $135+.

Sidestreet Salon in Woodstock is the lowest-priced close competitor I found: women’s shampoo/cut/style $32, men’s cut $19, kids cut $15, full colour $73, and full head foil with colour $101. This comes from a third-party business listing, so it is worth verifying by phone before using it as a pricing anchor.

## Positioning Notes

The local Oxford County/Tillsonburg price band appears meaningfully lower than London’s more specialized salons. A full-service Ingersoll salon could likely sit above Sidestreet and Tranquility if it emphasizes experience, consultation, colour quality, and modern blonding, but London pricing may feel high unless the brand is clearly premium.

For a competitive but healthy local menu, a plausible starting position would be:
- Women’s cut: $45-$55+
- Men’s cut: $28-$35+
- Kids cut: $18-$25+
- Root colour: $60-$75+
- Full colour: $75-$95+
- Partial highlights: $95-$125+
- Full highlights: $130-$160+
- Balayage: $180-$225+

Those numbers keep you out of the very cheapest lane while staying below many London specialist menus.
